    How to Hunt

    You are a Vulture. Something in this network is going bad. Spot the decay early and contain it before it spreads. Everything here is a simulation: shapes, particles and made-up names.

    The round

    1. Pick a Hunt (top right) and press Begin.
    2. Watch the map and The Watch log for amber and crimson.
    3. Click a node to open its dossier, then act.
    4. When the Hunt ends you get a stamped Case File with a grade, and can replay the round.

    Node states

    • Alive Healthy
    • Scented Suspicious: amber pulse
    • Rotting Compromised: crimson, cracks, vultures circling
    • Picked Clean Quarantined: ash gray in a cage
    • Revived Restored: white flash

    Actions

    Isolate
    Quarantine a node. Nothing passes through it. Press again to release.
    Block
    Blocklist the outside source. Its traffic bounces off the firewall.
    Restore
    Revive a Rotting node after 5 seconds.
    Trace
    Draw the path the rot took to reach this node.

    Each action has a cooldown, so choose well. Quarantined nodes cost a little at grading.

    Controls

    Mouse
    Drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom.
    Touch
    One finger to orbit, two to zoom and pan.
    Double-click
    Swoop down onto a node.
    Space / Esc
    Pause / close the dossier.
    M
    Ambient sound on or off: wind and distant wings (off by default).
